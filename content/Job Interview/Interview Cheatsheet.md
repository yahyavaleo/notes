## Behavioral Interview Question

| **Category**                  | **Question**                                   | **Tips**                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your Story**                | Tell me about yourself.                        | Focus on your professional journey, not personal details. Mention 2-3 relevant achievements and keep it brief, under 3 minutes.              |
| **Your Interest**             | Why are you interested in this role?           | Research the company and connect your goals to their mission. Highlight specific aspects of the role that excite you.                        |
| **Teamwork**                  | Tell me about a time you worked in a team.     | Focus on collaboration, mentioning how you contributed and helped others. Include a specific outcome the team achieved together.             |
| **Communication**             | How do you handle stressful situations?        | Mention specific techniques, like prioritizing tasks or taking breaks, and share a brief example of how you stayed calm under pressure.      |
| **Strengths**                 | What are your strengths?                       | Choose a strength that solves a problem the company faces and back it up with a quick story or example of how you have applied it.           |
| **Weaknesses**                | What is your greatest weakness?                | Choose a real weakness that would not affect your job performance and show how you are actively working to improve it.                       |
| **Leadership**                | Describe a time when you showed leadership.    | Share a time when you took charge, even if it was not your formal role. Focus on how you motivated others and the result of your leadership. |
| **Initiative**                | Give an example of a time you took initiative. | Share when you saw a problem and solved it without being asked. Show the impact of your actions and how it benefited the company.            |
| **Fit**                       | Why should we hire you?                        | Highlight how your skills solve a key problem the company is facing. Show enthusiasm and a desire to contribute to the team's success.       |
| **Problem-Solving Abilities** | Describe a time you faced a challenge at work. | Use the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Focus on the action you took and the positive outcome it led to.                       |
| **Problem-Solving Abilities** | How do you prioritize your work?               | Describe a system you use, like a to-do list or time-blocking. Show flexibility and how you adjust when things change.                       |
| **Long-Term Goals**           | Where do you see yourself in 5 years?          | Talk about career growth in a way that shows commitment to the role. Mention skills you are eager to develop that align with the job.        |

## Additional Tips
- **Research**: Go beyond the website. Check their recent news and social media too.
- **Practice**: Do not memorize. Rehearse enough to sound natural and confident.
- **Ask questions**: Have 3 insightful questions ready about the role, team, and culture.
- **Follow Up**: Send a short, thoughtful email within 24 hours of the interview.
